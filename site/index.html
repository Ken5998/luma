A different kind
of atmosphere.

From cool blue to warm plasma. Pick a palette and make it yours.

In Luma, choose which display shows the clock. Always top center. Always optional.

01

Every screen, its own flow.

Independent animation on each display, with its own resolution and scaling. One key or mouse movement brings you back.

02

Set your own pace.

Adjust speed, line size, and simulation quality. Your preferences stay with you through updates.

03

Time, when you want it.

A quiet 24-hour clock, centered at the top of your chosen display. Turn it on or leave the light alone.

A MOMENT TO SET UP

Unzip.
Install.
Drift.

Get Luma ↓
  1. 1

    Make a little room.

    Download and extract the ZIP. Close any running screensaver and the Windows preview.

  2. 2

    Install just for you.

    Run Install.cmd. No administrator rights needed. Select your wait time in Windows Screen Saver Settings and click Apply.

  3. 3

    Choose your atmosphere.

    Open Settings to pick a palette, tune the motion, and enable the clock. Changes apply on the next launch.

An independent project, built in the open. The package uses Windows PowerShell for setup and settings. Run Uninstall.cmd to remove Luma; your preferences are kept. Installation details ↗
